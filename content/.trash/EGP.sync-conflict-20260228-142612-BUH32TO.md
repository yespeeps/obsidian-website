### Science and tech
First of all, we must define science and technology themselves. Science is knowledge, discovery, and reasoning about the world that become theories and laws. Technology is the practical application of science for benefit of the human race.

To create theories and laws, humanity developed a scientific method:
1st step: Observation / hypotheses
2nd step: Experimentation / testing
3rd step: Evaluation + further research.
