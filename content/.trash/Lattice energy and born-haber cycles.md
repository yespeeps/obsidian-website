4. Factors affecting electron affinity
	1. greater nuclear charge (more protons) results in higher attraction betweennucleus and incoming electron -> electron affinity becomes more exothermic
		![[Lattice energy and born-haber cycles 2026-07-23 08.16.13.excalidraw]]
	2. atomic radius: smaller atoms have stronger attraction -> electron affinity becomes more exothermic ![[Lattice energy and born-haber cycles 2026-07-23 08.22.37.excalidraw]]
	3. electron shielding: more inner shells reduce attraction between nucleus and electron -> less  exothermic
5. Trends in groups 16 and 17
	1. EA less exothermic down the group
		1. increasing atomic radius
		2. increasing electron shielding
		3. weaker nuclear attraction
